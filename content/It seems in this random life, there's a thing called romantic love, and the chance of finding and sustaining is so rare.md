It seems in this random life, there's a thing called romantic love, and the chance of finding and sustaining is so rare, that I think we should celebrate when such a rare thing is found. Like we celebrate the birth of a child, or a miraculous successful surgery, it's so rare and so much is attributed to luck that it deserves a celebration.

To all the hopeless romantics out there (I, for one, am not), you should all be okay with the fact that such love is rare, and you may not see it in this life, but still it's worth searching for (or waiting for, for a while, not forever).

I am not a hopeless romantic, I like to call myself a realist romantic. I will search for such love, I will try my best to make such love last, but I will teach myself to be okay with the rareness of such love, and will teach myself to be okay with not finding such love in this lifetime. But still, accept any fraction of love I get and try my best to sustain it as much as I can, if it seems worth it.
