We get to live one life. Whatever we do becomes our story. I mean there's literally no going back. 

So let's get this clear, everyone is born the same way. The only thing that makes each person different is the situation, decisions, and all the things that happen around the person. But since the number of humans to ever live on earth is unimaginable, is it possible that someone in the past or in the future has already or will eventually live the same life as me? Has a person already made every choice I've ever made or will ever make? You know since there are a lot of humans. There should be a possibility that everything I said above makes sense, kinda?

In a sense, has there been a person that has lived my life or will live my life?

So does that mean the person that lived my life was me? So, does that mean I have already lived the full same life in the past? And I may live another life in the future?

Although I just realized the surrounding in the past and the future wasn't and won't be same in any way.

So that means I am living a completely new life.
