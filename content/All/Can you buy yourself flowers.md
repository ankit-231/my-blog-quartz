The name is clickbaity. But the real question is can you really live your life alone?

Now the obvious answer is no. We're social animals.

But we'll explore more of why here.

- We don't have answers to every question.
- We don't have experience in every field.
- We might not be able to burn a fire alone. Like that time I could not do the barbecue alone.

The title is inspired by Miley Cyrus's song I can buy myself flowers.
